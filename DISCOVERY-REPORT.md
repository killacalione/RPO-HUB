# Discovery synthesis

Updated for consolidation on 2026-10-15. The original October 14 research/interview report is preserved locally. This maintained summary excludes personal interview and funding context.

## Established direction

RPO HUB aims at code-created worlds with shared identity and extensible clients. Both local and integrated browser authoring are required. The first demo is exploration, personalization, portal travel and return with choices intact. Requested space visibility is selected at creation. The intended first online release includes public sharing, text/proximity/party voice, trusted direct publishing and all ages under reviewed age-group/country/contact policy.

## Actual current state

The current deliverable is the community foundation. No S1 SDK, sandbox, multiplayer, voice or production service is implemented. A previous static profile/hub/world prototype remains local as historical evidence. Read [work state](docs/planning/WORK-STATE.md), [roadmap](docs/planning/ROADMAP.md) and [S1 brief](docs/planning/S1-IMPLEMENTATION-BRIEF.md).

## Research conclusion

Use a small modular local experiment before operating a full social stack. Babylon/Monaco/QuickJS are candidate components; Hubs, Third Room/WebSG, Overte and Godot provide relevant prior art with different integration and operation costs. Interpreter interrupts, worker isolation, client volume controls and written policies do not by themselves establish hostile-runtime, media-denial or child-safety guarantees.
