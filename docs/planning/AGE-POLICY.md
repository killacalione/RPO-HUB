# Age-group communication proposal

**Requirement from round four:** Children hear/speak with their age group in public rooms. **Unknown:** Minimum supported child age, exact cohort boundaries, age-assurance/guardian process, applicability across party/text/indirect channels and each country's eligibility. This is an engineering/product proposal, not evidence of verified ages or legal compliance.

## Proposed smallest enforceable model

Serve the same published world in separate communication instances for eligible age cohorts. This reduces cross-group live names/avatars/signs/objects and voice leakage compared with mixed occupancy whose chat is merely hidden. Shared geometry with isolated channels is a later option only after every indirect communication path is governed. World listings and static creator content still need age/content/rights review.

Use an approved risk-based age/guardian process to produce a server-maintained eligibility decision and cohort grant. A client cannot change cohort/recipient lists during join. User-provided age may be part of a reviewed process where appropriate; it is not proof of a real person's age. Do not assume government-ID collection is universally required. Minimize retained attributes and do not publish birth dates/identity evidence. Exact assurance confidence, refresh/appeal and provider processing remain Unknown [S70].

Bind communication grants to subject/session, world instance or party, cohort, policy version, allowed actions and expiry. Unknown/expired eligibility receives no protected communication grant under the proposed policy. Check discovery, joins, text delivery, voice recipients, party invites/membership changes and reconnect; revoke active grants on block/guardian/moderation/eligibility changes. Trusted publishers cannot override the policy.

## Paths that also carry communication

Player names, avatar text, gestures, signs, editable objects, live world changes, scripts, external links and creator profiles can carry messages. Hiding chat or setting audio gain to zero is not age isolation. Govern these paths, expose only permitted participants, or use separate instances. Child parties that survive travel must keep the same eligibility/recipient rules. Family/teacher cross-age exceptions are not selected; do not add them silently.

## Evidence required

Define bands and regional eligibility; review actual assurance/parent/data flows. Test modified client age/cohort fields, token replay/expiry, unknown cohorts, adult join to child public room/party, changes after join, indirect message paths and denied voice tracks. An account-age field or frontend dropdown passing UI tests does not demonstrate actual-user age or hostile-client isolation. No age, parent or cohort enforcement has been implemented.

Worldwide consent, account eligibility and contact rules vary; cohort membership does not supply parental consent or country clearance. See [global readiness](../legal/GLOBAL-READINESS.md), [voice](VOICE-DESIGN.md) and [v0.1](V0.1-SPEC.md).

## Round-six owner visibility boundaries

Owners choose visibility, but cannot select a different cohort or override guardian/contact/region/content eligibility. Invite-only is not proof of actual ages or permission for cross-age contact. Proposed high-privacy initial setting follows applicable child-data review [S85]; one space dropdown is not complete privacy compliance. Live edits and object/name updates remain indirect communication. See [space access](SPACE-ACCESS.md).
